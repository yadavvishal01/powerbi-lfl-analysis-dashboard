# Like-for-Like Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## Project Summary

This Power BI project analyses like-for-like business performance by comparing current-year and previous-year results. The dashboard focuses on sales, transactions, average transaction value, quantity, regional performance, store formats, departments, and categories.

## Business Problem

The business needs to understand how its performance has changed compared with the previous year and identify the regions, stores, departments, and categories contributing to growth or decline.

## Key Work Performed

* Built a Power BI dashboard for current-year vs previous-year performance analysis.
* Calculated and analysed sales, transactions, average transaction value, and quantity.
* Analysed regional and city-level performance.
* Compared store formats based on sales and performance metrics.
* Analysed department-level performance against the previous year.
* Drilled down from departments into individual categories.
* Analysed monthly sales and growth trends.
* Used LFL growth metrics to compare comparable business performance.

## Important Insights / Findings

* Current-year sales were ₹92.7M compared with ₹77.4M in the previous year, representing 19.7% growth.
* Current-year transactions were 2,15,887 compared with 1,73,016 previously, representing 24.8% growth.
* Average transaction value decreased from 447 to 429, a 4.1% decline.
* Quantity increased from 474K to 640K, representing 35.1% growth.
* Among the listed regions, North recorded ₹23.8M in current-year sales with 19.6% LFL growth.
* Household recorded the highest department-level sales growth shown at 32.4%.
* Sugar & Salt recorded 44.0% sales LFL growth among the listed categories.
* Supermarket contributed the largest share of sales among the displayed store formats.

## Business Impact / Interpretation

The dashboard helps identify where business growth is coming from and where performance is declining compared with the previous year. The increase in sales, transactions, and quantity alongside the decline in average transaction value provides a useful view of changes in customer purchasing behaviour. Regional, store-format, department, and category analysis can support further investigation into business performance.
